On-chain controls

Authorities are disclosed explicitly. Verify them against a Solana explorer.

Freeze authority revoked

The CENT mint has no freeze authority. Token accounts cannot be frozen through that authority.

Mint authority active

Mint authority remains active through the project's Solana program. Project policy is to mint only after corresponding physical pennies are received; this is a policy, not an immutable supply cap.

Upgradeable program

Minting program: 8ut4mptpdzQ7qkEfuVsiT5QR9kADKZTszcpKCYmmfaXg. The program remains upgradeable. Its upgrade authority is a Squads vault governed by a 2-of-3 voting threshold.

Mutable metadata

Token metadata remains mutable. The same 2-of-3 Squads vault controls program upgrades and metadata updates. Vault authority: A5v2A5mwwM7a2S3JX2h9aHQ7BSBQEoje5TesFWFE3tUy.

2-of-3 voter approvals

The controlling Squads v4 account requires two approvals from three voting members. Individual member addresses are intentionally not republished here; the threshold and permissions can be verified on-chain.

Liquidity is multisig-controlled, not locked

All four current Raydium CENT/USDC concentrated-liquidity position NFTs are held by the same Squads vault. Withdrawing or repositioning project liquidity therefore follows the 2-of-3 approval path. The positions are not time-locked and may be rebalanced when needed.
